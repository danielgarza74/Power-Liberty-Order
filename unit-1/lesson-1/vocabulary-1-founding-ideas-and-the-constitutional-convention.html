Part 1 · Match the Meaning

Read the meanings. Then pick a letter for each term.

MEANINGS
  • A The idea that the people are the source of a government’s power and the government must answer to them.
  • B The system in which each state’s electors, not voters directly, choose the President.
  • C An imagined time with no government or laws. Thinkers use it to ask why people need rules.
  • D People who opposed the Constitution because they feared a strong national government would take away liberty.
  • E A loose union of states where the states keep most of the power and the central government is weak.
  • F Formal approval. The Constitution needed approval from nine of the thirteen states to take effect.
  • G An agreement in which people give up some freedom to a government so it will keep them safe.
  • H A 1786–87 uprising of Massachusetts farmers who closed courts. Congress could not stop it, so leaders wanted a stronger government.
  • I People who supported the Constitution and wanted a stronger national government.
  • J Splitting a government into branches so no one person or group holds all the power.
  • K Rights people are born with, like life and liberty, that no government should take away.
  • L The Convention deal that made a House based on population and a Senate with two senators for each state.

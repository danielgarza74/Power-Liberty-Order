UNIT 2

Interactions Among Branches of Government

Photo: Architect of the Capitol / Wikimedia Commons, Public domain

Unit overview

Unit 2 examines the three branches and the bureaucracy as they actually operate. You will see how the structure of Congress reflects a compromise between state interests and national needs, how a president leads inside constitutional limits, how the Supreme Court acts as final arbiter of the Constitution, and how the federal bureaucracy carries out policy that touches daily life.

In this unit

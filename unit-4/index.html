Unit overview

Unit 4 looks at how citizens' political beliefs are formed and how those beliefs reach government. It covers public opinion and political socialization, political parties and interest groups as linkage institutions, the mechanics of elections and campaigns, and the media's roles as gatekeeper and watchdog.
